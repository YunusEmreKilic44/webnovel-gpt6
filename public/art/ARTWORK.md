# Satir Anime Artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. Images are original
illustrations, not covers from existing published manga. The original generated
files remain in the Codex generated_images directory; these copies ship with the app.
Next.js Image optimizes the PNG assets for the reader's device.

## Prompts

hero.png:
Create a premium anime / manhwa illustration for the full width hero background
of a Turkish fantasy webnovel reading website. Wide landscape 3:1. An original
adult dark-haired male swordsman in black flowing cloak, three-quarter profile,
looking at viewer, on the RIGHT third of composition, with a luminous crimson
blade, detailed illustrated face, silver clasps and red ribbons. Background
sprawling ancient black-stone eastern fantasy city, red sun, floating embers,
intricate ink hatching, dramatic cinematic lighting, crisp colored manga cover
art, charcoal black, vivid crimson, subtle teal. The LEFT half is quiet scenic
negative space with black ink shadows to support white website title overlay.
Not a mockup. No text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark. Full bleed professional
anime key art, beautifully drawn, sharp, NOT photorealistic.

The six cover prompts use this template:
Create ONE portrait 2:3 original anime manhwa webnovel cover illustration.
[SUBJECT]. Beautiful intricate Japanese ink linework, expressive sophisticated
faces, dynamic framing, high quality colored manga art, detailed scenic
background, premium light novel illustration, sharp cel shading. Image only,
no words, no typography, no watermark, no borders. Full bleed.

- ember.png: adult silver-haired warrior prince holding a glowing red sword in a ruined imperial throne room, crimson and charcoal.
- ocean.png: adult female navigator with short dark hair and a long ivory coat, on the deck of an airship over a turquoise ocean of clouds, teal and silver.
- forest.png: adult elven woman archer with emerald eyes and long dark hair in an ancient overgrown magical forest, jade green and gold.
- violet.png: adult black-haired detective in a raincoat beneath neon signs in a futuristic Tokyo street at night, electric purple and cyan.
- sand.png: adult desert wanderer with flowing white hair and a black cloak, ornate blade, huge gold eclipse over ancient city, gold and black.
- rose.png: adult woman with flowing pink hair and an adult dark-haired man in academy uniforms on a rooftop under cherry blossom trees, expressive romantic composition, pink and sky blue.
